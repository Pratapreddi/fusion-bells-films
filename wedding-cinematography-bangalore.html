Film Philosophy

Not just a wedding video — a cinematic heirloom.

There is a world of difference between a typical wedding video and a cinema-grade wedding film. A video simply logs events on a tripod. A film evokes the exact emotional resonance of being there — the shiver in a voice, the laughter in the courtyard, and the swell of the shehnai.

“We record the speeches in full. The live acoustic vows. The exact second a brother's voice breaks. Nothing is staged, because the film is only ever as honest as your day was.”

Every frame is individually colour-graded in-house in DaVinci Resolve. We reject harsh digital sharpness in favour of a warm, organic filmic palette that honours your skin tones and wedding décor.

Every wedding is a feature film
waiting to be cut.

We capture the audio, the movement, and the heartbeat of your celebration —
two crafts, one crew, one shared creative obsession.

Common Questions

Wedding Cinematography FAQs

What types of wedding films do you offer?
We offer four formats: Teaser (60–90 sec, delivered in 5–7 days), Highlight Reel (3–5 min cinematic edit), Full Documentary (20–45 min with live audio), and Same-Day Edit (screened at your reception). Most couples choose the Highlight Reel + Teaser combination.
Do you shoot in 4K?
Yes, all our wedding films are shot in 4K resolution using cinema-grade cameras. This gives us the flexibility to crop, stabilise, and reframe in post-production without any loss of quality.
How long does it take to receive the final wedding film?
Teasers: 5–7 days. Highlight reels: 6–8 weeks. Full documentaries: 8–10 weeks. Same-day edits are screened at your reception. Rush delivery is available for teasers and highlights at an additional fee.
Do you record the wedding audio?
Absolutely. We use wireless lavalier microphones on the couple and the officiant, plus a dedicated audio recorder near the sound system. Every vow, speech, and blessing is captured in crystal-clear audio.
Can we choose the music for our wedding film?
Yes, we encourage it. Share songs meaningful to your relationship, or we'll score the film from our curated library of licensed tracks. The final music is always approved by you before delivery.
