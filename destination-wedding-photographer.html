The Destination Journey

A celebration that everyone will remember.

A destination wedding is an immersive celebration. When your guests fly to a lakeside palace in Udaipur, a beach resort in South Goa, or a cliffside in Bali, they are committing three days of their lives to your love. The photographs and films need to match the extraordinary scale of that commitment.

“We don't just show up to take photos. We arrive a day early, scout the natural lighting transitions, coordinate with your wedding planner, and capture the full spirit of your destination.”

Our destination crew manages everything independently — flights, local ground logistics, gear redundancy, and multi-day parallel event schedules — so you can immerse yourself completely in your celebration.

Common Questions

Destination Wedding FAQs

What is included in a destination wedding package?
Our packages include return flights and accommodation for the full crew (2–5 members), multi-day coverage of all events, both photography and cinematography, hand-curated gallery, highlight reel, and full documentary film. Gear, insurance, and backup equipment are all included.
How far in advance should we book?
We recommend booking 6–12 months in advance for destination weddings, especially during peak season (October–March for India, April–June for international). This gives us time to scout locations, plan shot lists, and arrange travel.
Do you do a location recce before the wedding?
For Indian destinations, we arrive a day early to scout the venue and coordinate with décor teams. For international destinations, we do a detailed virtual recce via video call with the venue coordinator and your planner.
Which destination venues have you shot at?
We've covered weddings at Oberoi Udaivilas and City Palace in Udaipur, Taj Fort Aguada in Goa, Kumarakom Lake Resort in Kerala, Umaid Bhawan Palace in Jodhpur, heritage havelis in Jaipur, beach resorts in Bali and Thailand, and desert locations in Dubai.
Can you handle both photography and videography at a destination?
Absolutely — this is our specialty. One studio handling both eliminates coordination headaches, especially at destinations where logistics are complex. Our teams work together with a shared creative vision.
