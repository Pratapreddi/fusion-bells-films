The Session

More than just photos — your first visual chapter.

A pre-wedding photoshoot with Fusion Bells Films isn't about stiff poses in borrowed outfits at an artificial studio set. It is an editorial exploration of your relationship — capturing the way you laugh together, whisper private jokes, and hold each other when nobody is watching.

“We shoot during golden hour — the quiet hour after sunrise at Nandi Hills or the rich evening light filtering through the ancient trees of Lalbagh.”

Our sessions are unhurried, collaborative, and fun. It also serves as the ideal rehearsal for your wedding day — helping you feel effortlessly relaxed and confident in front of our lenses.

The Dubai Feature

A desert, two people, and the golden hour.

Looking for something breathtaking? We frequently direct pre-wedding film and photo sessions across destination landscapes — from the sweeping golden dunes of Dubai to the ancient granite boulders of Hampi and the mist-covered tea gardens of Coorg.

Every session includes high-resolution photography, an optional 2–3 minute cinematic teaser film scored to music, and drone aerial cinematography where permitted.

Common Questions

Pre-Wedding Photoshoot FAQs

What are the best pre-wedding shoot locations in Bangalore?
Popular locations include Nandi Hills (sunrise golden hour), Lalbagh Botanical Garden, Cubbon Park, Bangalore Palace, Ulsoor Lake, Hesaraghatta Lake, and the heritage streets of Basavanagudi. For couples willing to travel, we also recommend Coorg, Hampi, Mysuru Palace, and Gokarna beach.
How much does a pre-wedding photoshoot cost in Bangalore?
Packages start from ₹25,000 for a half-day session (4 hours, single location) and go up to ₹1,50,000+ for multi-location or destination sessions with video. Contact us for a personalised quote based on your concept.
How should we prepare for our pre-wedding shoot?
We recommend 2–3 outfit changes (coordinate colours, avoid busy prints), comfortable footwear for walking between spots, and arriving with a relaxed mindset. We send a detailed preparation guide after booking.
Do you also shoot pre-wedding videos?
Yes! We offer combined photo + video packages. The video is a 2–3 minute cinematic short film set to music and hand colour-graded — perfect for sharing at the sangeet or on social media.
Can we do a pre-wedding shoot outside Bangalore?
Absolutely. We regularly shoot at Goa, Udaipur, Jaipur, Kerala, Ooty, Hampi, and international locations like Dubai, Bali, and Thailand. Travel and accommodation for our crew are included in destination packages.
